# Failure Modes & Limitations

## 1. Vision Model Hallucination

**Risk**: The vision LLM may report findings not present on the X-ray.

**Detection**: Hallucination guard flags out-of-scope findings (skull, abdomen, pelvis).

**Limitation**: It cannot detect false-positive findings inside the chest anatomy.

**Mitigation**: Confidence thresholds + physician veto + visual grounding requirement (attention regions).

---

## 2. Lab-Image Contradiction Blind Spots

**Risk**: Image shows pneumonia but labs are normal, or vice versa.

**Detection**: 5 explicit contradiction rules (pneumonia without leukocytosis, edema without hypoxia, etc.).

**Limitation**: Rules are manually curated, not exhaustive.

**Mitigation**: Safety panel surfaces contradictions with confidence penalties. Unknown contradictions default to "proceed with caution."

---

## 3. Demographic Bias

**Risk**: AI models underperform for underrepresented populations.

**Detection**: Bias auditor flags specific demographics for high-confidence cardiomegaly findings.

**Limitation**: Only 2 rules implemented (race + sex). Does not cover age, BMI, geographic, or genetic factors.

**Mitigation**: Flag + lower confidence for triggered demographics. Full transparency in audit log.

---

## 4. Model Consistency (Non-Determinism)

**Risk**: Vision/text LLMs produce variable outputs for identical inputs.

**Experience**: ~95% consistency observed for static inputs on warm KV cache.

**Limitation**: First inference (cold KV cache) may differ from warmed runs.

**Mitigation**: Contingency cache stores pre-computed outputs for the 6 demo cases.

---

## 5. Pediatric Patients

**Risk**: Models are trained on adult chest X-rays only.

**Detection**: Pediatric safety gate triggers for age < 18.

**Limitation**: No alternative model for pediatrics. Gate only warns, does not block.

**Mitigation**: Manual review mandatory for pediatric cases (documented in UI).

---

## 6. No Treatment Protocol Integration

**Risk**: Suggested actions are generated by general-purpose LLM, not verified against institutional protocols.

**Limitation**: ClinSight does not integrate with EHR order sets or hospital-specific clinical pathways.

**Mitigation**: All actions are "suggestions" requiring physician approval.

---

## 7. Network/Server Failure

**Risk**: Droplet destroyed or vLLM servers crash.

**Detection**: Health check (`/health`) + contingency fallback module.

**Mitigation**: Mock client returns deterministic outputs in < 0.2s. Contingency module returns safe default (ESI 3) with manual-review flag.

---

## 8. Regulatory Non-Compliance

**Risk**: Not FDA-cleared. Not CE-marked. Not a diagnostic device.

**Documented**: Medical disclaimer on every output. Physician-in-the-loop requirement.

**Limitation**: Cannot be deployed for clinical decision-making without regulatory approval.
